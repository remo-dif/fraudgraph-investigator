# Product Vision

FraudGraph Network Audit is an internal service for retrospective analysis of historical business datasets. An authorised analyst selects an isolated dataset, starts with a known identifier, traces related activity, and exports evidence another analyst can reproduce.

The first workflow is import and validate, search, inspect paths and source records, compare cluster exposure by currency, export, then delete. Observed event participation, inferred links and analyst-confirmed conclusions must be visibly distinct. An exposure estimate is separate from confirmed loss.

Rewarded gaming, research panels, loyalty, affiliate marketing, promotion abuse and digital platforms are market hypotheses. Validate data availability, investigation usefulness and repeatability with synthetic exercises before making commercial claims.

Success means completing the workflow in Compose with reproducible synthetic evidence and verified access and deletion controls. Real customer data remains prohibited until the security gates pass. Billing, global identity matching, crawling, biometrics, payment interception, blocking, ML pipelines and distributed infrastructure are outside scope.
